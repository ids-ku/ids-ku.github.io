Led by [Jungha Kim]({{ '/people/#member-kim-jh' | relative_url }})

This research concerns **omnimodal world models for Physical AI** and **efficient robot-policy execution on edge devices**. The systems study aims to reduce the time from sensor input to robot commands while preserving numerical accuracy and input freshness.

## Current Focus

The study targets dual-camera policy execution on **Jetson Orin Nano 8GB**, with **SO-ARM101** as the evaluation platform. Planned comparisons use PyTorch and TensorRT-based baselines to assess execution bottlenecks and the effect of optimization.

Planned evaluation covers robot responsiveness, the contribution of runtime and GPU kernel optimizations, and differences across power modes and GPU environments.
